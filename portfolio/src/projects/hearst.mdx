# Paid Media at Hearst

As a Paid Media Manager at Hearst Global, I run campaigns across Meta, TikTok and YouTube for magazines including Harper’s Bazaar, ELLE and Esquire.

Across these titles, I work on brand campaigns for the likes of Valentino, Apple and Land Rover, managing budgets exceeding £100k.

My role combines audience strategy, campaign performance and creative thinking. I oversee how budgets are allocated across platforms and work closely with our content team, Hearst Made, using performance insights to inform creative decisions and help brands make the most of Hearst’s reach and editorial credibility.
