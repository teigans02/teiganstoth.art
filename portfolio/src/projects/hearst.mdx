# Hearst

As a Paid Media Manager at Hearst Global, I run paid campaigns across Meta, TikTok and YouTube for titles including Harper’s Bazaar, ELLE and Esquire.

I work on partnerships with brands such as Valentino, Apple and Land Rover, managing budgets from £10k to over £100k.

My role combines audience strategy, campaign performance and creative thinking. I manage how budgets are spent across platforms and work closely with our content team, Hearst Made, using performance insights to shape creative decisions and help brands make the most of Hearst’s reach and editorial credibility.
